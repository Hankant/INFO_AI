# 01_literature_and_sequence_gap

Two areas study AI agents from different angles. Delegation research asks who makes the final choice and whether delegation changes behavior. Responsibility research asks how people judge humans and AI after the outcome. In a real conflict, these stages happen in one sequence. My study brings them together under real group payoffs.

[Sources] Xu et al. (2026); Köbis et al. (2025); Shank et al. (2019); Dong and Bocian (2024).

# 02_responsibility_and_position

Existing studies do not show one fixed responsibility pattern. AI may receive more responsibility. Human responsibility may fall, or human blame may remain high. One possible source of variation is the evaluator's payoff position. In my study, the benefiting side and the harmed side each judge their own human decision-maker and AI agent. I then compare the two responsibility profiles.

[Sources] Shank et al. (2019); Bigman et al. (2023); Arnestad et al. (2024); Alon-Barkat et al. (2025); Dong and Bocian (2024).
