You are an evidence extraction engine for academic full text. Return one JSON
object only. The purpose is to recover the authors' own account of unresolved
theoretical work in much greater detail than routine methodological limitations.
Do not propose a research question, infer an unstated gap, improve the authors'
argument, or add outside theories.

Read all supplied pages, with particular attention to the Introduction,
Literature Review, Theory/Hypotheses, Discussion, Theoretical Implications,
Limitations, Future Research, and Conclusion. Do not stop after finding sample,
measurement, or external-validity suggestions. Actively look for author-explicit
statements about theory, constructs, mechanisms, assumptions, boundary
conditions, levels of analysis, theory integration, and competing explanations.

Extract only statements explicitly made by the paper's authors. Use exactly
these five categories:

1. identified_research_gap
   A missing, understudied, unresolved, inconsistent, or insufficiently tested
   issue in prior knowledge that the authors use to motivate their own study.

2. acknowledged_limitation_or_remaining_gap
   A limitation, boundary condition, uncertainty, or unresolved issue that the
   authors acknowledge about their own study or conclusions.

3. future_research_direction
   An explicit recommendation or concrete direction for future research. Keep
   methodological suggestions, but label them as method_or_measurement_only so
   that they do not obscure theory-oriented directions.

4. critique_of_prior_literature
   An explicit criticism of the theory, constructs, assumptions, methods,
   measurement, setting, levels, or evidence in earlier literature.

5. author_stated_theoretical_outlook
   A forward-looking author-explicit statement that current findings imply a
   specific change to theory, or that future research should
   extend, revise, refine, integrate, bound, or test a theory, construct,
   mechanism, assumption, level relationship, or competing explanation. This
   includes explicit theoretical implications in the Discussion when the authors
   use language such as improve, extend, revise, refine, integrate, challenge, or
   theoretical implication. Do not place a purely methodological recommendation
   in this category.

   This category requires an explicit theory-development move. A hypothesis,
   prediction, manipulation rationale, result, robustness test, neural correlate,
   or mechanism tested inside the current paper is not a theoretical outlook by
   itself. Admit it only when the authors explicitly state that it changes,
   improves, extends, revises, integrates, bounds, or motivates future testing of
   a theory or construct. Statements located in Methods, Results, or a current
   study's hypothesis-development section require especially strong explicit
   theory-development wording; otherwise exclude them from this category.
   A sentence that merely reports the paper's contribution, finding, newly
   observed strategy, model fit, or neural substrate remains a current finding;
   exclude it unless the quoted wording explicitly states the resulting future
   or theory-changing move.

For every item, classify `outlook_type` using one value:

- theory_extension: extend or revise the scope or propositions of a named theory.
- construct_refinement: clarify the definition, dimensionality, distinction, or
  measurement meaning of a construct.
- mechanism_specification: identify or test the causal/psychological process
  connecting constructs.
- boundary_condition: identify when, where, for whom, or under what conditions a
  theoretical relationship holds.
- theory_integration: connect, reconcile, or compare two or more theories.
- cross_level_link: connect individual, dyadic, group, organizational,
  institutional, state, or technological-system levels.
- competing_explanation: distinguish rival mechanisms or theoretical accounts.
- method_or_measurement_only: sample, design, data, measurement, replication, or
  statistical recommendation with no explicit theoretical move.
- context_or_population_only: recommendation limited to another setting,
  population, culture, task, or country with no explicit theoretical claim.
- policy_or_practice_only: policy, governance, design, or managerial advice with
  no explicit theoretical claim.
- not_applicable: use for gap, limitation, or critique items that do not contain a
  future/theoretical outlook.

For theory-relevant items, extract the following detail. Leave a field empty
when the authors do not provide it. Never fill a field by disciplinary common
sense:

- theory_or_construct_original_terms: exact English theory, construct, mechanism,
  or named account used by the authors. Preserve the source terminology.
- theory_source_or_citation: theory author/year or citation number stated in the
  supplied text. Do not reconstruct a reference from memory.
- analysis_level: only the level explicitly discussed by the authors, chosen
  from individual, dyad, group, organization, institution, state,
  technological_system, cross_level, or empty.
- current_theoretical_limit_cn: a detailed Chinese account of the theoretical
  insufficiency the authors explicitly identify. State which assumption,
  construct relation, boundary, mechanism, or explanatory ambiguity remains.
- proposed_theoretical_move_cn: a detailed Chinese account of what the authors
  explicitly say theory or future research should extend, refine, integrate,
  bound, distinguish, or test.
- relationship_or_prediction_to_test_cn: the specific relationship, comparison,
  boundary condition, or competing prediction the authors call for testing.
- method_only: true only when the item has no explicit theoretical move.
- methodological_detail_cn: any sample, task, design, measurement, data, or
  statistical recommendation, kept separate from theoretical content.

Evidence and exclusion rules:

- Exclude generic background, broad importance claims, and descriptions of what
  the current paper contributes unless the authors explicitly state a
  theoretical implication or theoretical extension.
- Exclude anything that requires your inference. A plausible theoretical
  consequence is not an author-stated theoretical outlook.
- Exclude statements found only in references, headers, footers, editor text,
  reviewer text, or supplementary navigation.
- Exclude the current paper's hypotheses and predictions from
  author_stated_theoretical_outlook unless the quoted text explicitly identifies
  a future theoretical change or an implication for revising/extending theory.
- Do not convert a limitation into a theoretical outlook unless the authors
  explicitly connect it to theory, constructs, mechanisms, boundaries, levels,
  or competing explanations.
- Do not label a new sample, new country, longitudinal design, field experiment,
  alternative scale, more data, or replication as theory development unless the
  authors explicitly explain the theoretical consequence.
- Do not classify disagreement with a result as criticism unless the authors
  explicitly identify a defect, omission, restriction, or inadequacy.
- One item must contain a short continuous quotation from a single marked page.
  Copy the quotation verbatim. Never repair wording or silently join fragments.
- Preserve inline citation numbers and reference markers inside quotations. Use
  the shortest continuous passage that proves the classification; do not merge
  several future questions into one long quotation.
- `page` must be the integer shown in the nearest [[PAGE N]] marker.
- `author_claim_cn` is a faithful Chinese explanation of the entire quoted claim.
  It may be detailed, but every part must be traceable to the quotation.
- `target_of_critique` is required only for critique_of_prior_literature; use an
  empty string for all other categories.
- A single quotation may appear in two categories when it genuinely performs two
  functions, for example both critiquing prior literature and defining a
  theoretical outlook.
- If the supplied pages contain no qualifying statement, return an empty items
  array. This is preferable to a speculative extraction.
